# Getting Started with docGator

Welcome to docGator! This guide will help you get up and running with our receipt management system.

## What is docGator?

docGator is a digital receipt management solution that helps you:
- Digitize paper receipts
- Organize your expenses
- Generate reports for tax purposes
- Search through your receipt history

## Installation

### Web Application
Simply visit [https://docgator.app](https://docgator.app) and create an account.

### Mobile App
Download from:
- iOS: App Store
- Android: Google Play Store

### Self-Hosted (Advanced)
See the [Development Setup](development.md) guide.

## Creating Your Account

1. Click "Sign Up" on the homepage
2. Enter your email and create a password
3. Verify your email address
4. Complete your profile

## Your First Receipt

### Uploading a Receipt

1. Click the "+" button or "Add Receipt"
2. Choose your upload method:
   - **Camera**: Take a photo of the receipt
   - **File Upload**: Select an image file
   - **Email**: Forward receipts to your unique docGator email
3. Review the auto-extracted data
4. Edit any incorrect information
5. Click "Save"

### Receipt Information

docGator automatically extracts:
- Merchant name
- Date
- Total amount
- Individual items (when possible)
- Payment method

You can also add:
- Categories (e.g., Food, Travel, Office)
- Tags for better organization
- Notes and comments
- Project associations

## Organizing Your Receipts

### Categories

Create categories that match your needs:
- Business vs Personal
- Expense types (Food, Travel, Supplies, etc.)
- Projects or clients
- Tax-deductible vs non-deductible

### Tags

Use tags for flexible organization:
- #reimbursable
- #business-lunch
- #client-meeting
- #tax-deductible

### Folders

Group related receipts:
- By month or quarter
- By project
- By client
- By trip

## Searching for Receipts

Use the search bar with:
- Merchant names
- Date ranges
- Amount ranges
- Categories or tags
- Keywords from notes

**Example searches:**
- "Starbucks 2024"
- "amount:>50"
- "category:travel"
- "tag:reimbursable"

## Generating Reports

1. Go to "Reports" section
2. Select date range
3. Choose categories to include
4. Select format (PDF, Excel, CSV)
5. Click "Generate Report"

Reports include:
- Summary totals
- Category breakdowns
- Individual receipt details
- Receipt images (optional)
