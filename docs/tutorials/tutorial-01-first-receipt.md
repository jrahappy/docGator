# Tutorial 1: Creating Your First Receipt

**Estimated time**: 10 minutes  
**Difficulty**: Beginner

## What You'll Learn

- How to upload your first receipt
- Understanding auto-extracted data
- Editing receipt information
- Adding categories and tags

## Prerequisites

- A docGator account (create one at [docgator.app](https://docgator.app))
- A receipt image (paper receipt photo or digital receipt)

## Step 1: Log In to docGator

1. Go to [https://docgator.app](https://docgator.app)
2. Click **Log In** in the top right
3. Enter your credentials
4. You'll land on the Dashboard

## Step 2: Navigate to Add Receipt

From the Dashboard, you have multiple options:
- Click the large **"+ Add Receipt"** button
- Click the **"+"** icon in the navigation bar
- Use keyboard shortcut: `Ctrl+N` (Windows) or `Cmd+N` (Mac)

The "Add Receipt" dialog will open.

## Step 3: Upload Your Receipt

Choose your upload method:

### Option A: Upload from File
1. Click **"Choose File"** or drag-and-drop
2. Select your receipt image
3. Click **Open**

### Option B: Take a Photo
1. Click **"Camera"** tab
2. Allow camera access if prompted
3. Position the receipt in the frame
4. Click the **capture** button
5. Review the image and click **Use Photo**

### Option C: Email Forward
1. Click **"Email"** tab
2. Copy your unique receipt email address
3. Forward your digital receipt to this address
4. Return to docGator after a few moments

For this tutorial, let's use **Option A** with a sample receipt.

## Step 4: Review Auto-Extracted Data

After upload, docGator processes the receipt using OCR. Within a few seconds, you'll see:

```
✓ Processing complete

Merchant: Starbucks Coffee
Date: 2024-03-15
Total: $12.75
Items:
  - Grande Latte     $5.25
  - Croissant        $4.50
  - Tax              $3.00
```

**Note**: OCR isn't perfect. Always review the extracted data.

## Step 5: Edit Receipt Information

Let's verify and correct the data:

1. **Verify Merchant Name**
   - Field shows: "Starbucks Coffee"
   - Correct? ✓ Leave as is
   - Wrong? Click to edit

2. **Verify Date**
   - Field shows: "2024-03-15"
   - Use the date picker if incorrect

3. **Verify Total Amount**
   - Field shows: "$12.75"
   - Update if OCR misread it

4. **Review Items** (Optional)
   - Items are auto-detected when possible
   - Edit, add, or remove items as needed
   - Notice the tax is listed as an item - let's fix that

5. **Fix Tax Amount**
   - The tax ($3.00) was misidentified
   - Delete the tax "item"
   - Scroll down to **Tax Amount** field
   - Enter: $0.98 (the actual tax)
   - The subtotal auto-updates to $9.75

## Step 6: Add a Category

Categories help organize receipts:

1. Click the **Category** dropdown
2. Select **"Food & Dining"**
3. Or create new: Type "Coffee" and press Enter

For this example, choose **"Food & Dining"**.

## Step 7: Add Tags

Tags provide flexible organization:

1. Click in the **Tags** field
2. Type a tag name: "coffee"
3. Press **Enter** to add
4. Add more tags:
   - "morning" (Enter)
   - "cafe" (Enter)

Tags are helpful for:
- `#reimbursable` - Expense to be reimbursed
- `#personal` vs `#business`
- `#tax-deductible`
- Location or event tags

## Step 8: Add Notes (Optional)

Notes provide context:

1. Click in the **Notes** field
2. Add helpful information:
   ```
   Morning coffee break before client meeting.
   Met with Sarah from Acme Corp.
   ```

Notes are searchable, so include relevant keywords.

## Step 9: Set Payment Method (Optional)

Track how you paid:

1. Click **Payment Method** dropdown
2. Select method:
   - Credit Card (default)
   - Debit Card
   - Cash
   - Check
   - Other

## Step 10: Save the Receipt

1. Review all information one last time:
   - ✓ Merchant correct
   - ✓ Date correct
   - ✓ Amount correct
   - ✓ Category assigned
   - ✓ Tags added

2. Click **Save Receipt**

3. Success! You'll see:
   ```
   ✓ Receipt saved successfully
   ```

You'll be returned to your Dashboard, where your new receipt appears.

## Step 11: View Your Receipt

1. Find your receipt in the list
2. Click on it to open
3. Notice all the information is saved
4. The receipt image is stored

You can now:
- **Edit**: Click the edit icon
- **Delete**: Click the delete icon (moves to trash)
- **Share**: Click the share icon
- **Export**: Download as PDF

## Step 12: Practice with More Receipts

Try uploading different types of receipts:
- Restaurant receipts
- Gas station receipts
- Online shopping receipts (PDF)
- Hotel receipts
- Office supply receipts

Each type might extract differently, so practice reviewing and correcting.

## Tips & Tricks

### For Better OCR Results
- Use good lighting when photographing
- Lay receipt flat
- Include entire receipt in frame
- Avoid shadows and glare

### Organization Tips
- Add receipts immediately after purchase
- Use consistent category names
- Create a tagging strategy
- Review and clean up weekly

### Quick Actions
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: 
  - `Ctrl/Cmd + N`: New receipt
  - `Ctrl/Cmd + S`: Save
  - `Esc`: Cancel
- **Bulk Upload**: Upload multiple receipts at once
- **Email Integration**: Forward receipts automatically
