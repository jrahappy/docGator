# Basic Concepts

Understanding these core concepts will help you use docGator more effectively.

## Receipt Management Fundamentals

### What is a Receipt?

A receipt is proof of a transaction that typically includes:
- **Merchant**: Who you paid
- **Date**: When the transaction occurred
- **Amount**: How much you paid
- **Items**: What you purchased
- **Payment Method**: How you paid

### Why Manage Receipts?

- **Tax Purposes**: Document deductible expenses
- **Reimbursement**: Submit expense claims
- **Budgeting**: Track spending patterns
- **Warranty Claims**: Proof of purchase
- **Business Records**: Maintain accurate financial records

## Core Features

### Digital Storage

docGator stores receipt images and data securely in the cloud:
- **Automatic Backup**: Never lose a receipt
- **OCR Processing**: Text extraction from images
- **Data Security**: Encrypted storage
- **Accessibility**: Access from any device

### Organization System

#### Categories
Broad classifications for receipts:
- Pre-defined: Food, Travel, Office, Utilities, etc.
- Custom: Create your own categories
- Hierarchical: Sub-categories for detailed organization

**Example Category Structure:**
```
Business Expenses
├── Travel
│   ├── Airfare
│   ├── Hotels
│   └── Meals
├── Office Supplies
└── Client Entertainment
```

#### Tags
Flexible labels for cross-cutting concerns:
- Multiple tags per receipt
- Quick filtering
- Dynamic creation
- Color coding

#### Folders
Collections of receipts:
- Project-based grouping
- Time-period collections
- Client-specific folders

### Search and Filter

Find receipts using:
- **Text Search**: Merchant names, item descriptions
- **Date Filters**: Specific dates or ranges
- **Amount Filters**: Greater than, less than, exact
- **Category Filters**: Single or multiple
- **Tag Filters**: Any combination
- **Advanced Queries**: Complex search expressions

### Reports and Analytics

Transform receipt data into insights:
- **Expense Reports**: Summarize spending
- **Category Analysis**: Where money goes
- **Trend Charts**: Spending over time
- **Tax Reports**: Deductible expenses
- **Export Options**: PDF, Excel, CSV

## Data Fields

### Required Fields
- Merchant name
- Date
- Total amount

### Optional Fields
- Individual items and prices
- Tax amount
- Payment method
- Category
- Tags
- Notes
- Project/Client
- Location

### Auto-Extracted Fields
docGator uses OCR to automatically extract:
- Merchant name (90%+ accuracy)
- Date (95%+ accuracy)
- Total amount (85%+ accuracy)
- Items (70%+ accuracy)

Always review auto-extracted data for accuracy.

## Best Practices

### Capture Receipts Immediately
- Take photos right after purchases
- Set up email forwarding for online receipts
- Use mobile app for on-the-go capture

### Review Extracted Data
- Check for OCR errors
- Verify amounts and dates
- Add missing information

### Use Consistent Categorization
- Establish a category system
- Stick to your system
- Document category definitions

### Add Context
- Use notes for additional details
- Tag for special purposes
- Link to projects or clients

### Regular Maintenance
- Review receipts weekly
- Generate monthly reports
- Archive old receipts
- Update categories as needed

## Workflow Examples

### Business Expense Workflow
1. Receive receipt
2. Capture in docGator
3. Verify extracted data
4. Categorize as business expense
5. Tag as #reimbursable (if applicable)
6. Add to current month's folder
7. Generate monthly report for accounting

### Personal Budget Workflow
1. Capture all receipts
2. Categorize by expense type
3. Review weekly spending
4. Generate monthly category reports
5. Compare to budget
6. Adjust spending as needed

### Tax Preparation Workflow
1. Tag tax-deductible receipts throughout year
2. Create categories matching tax forms
3. Add notes about deductibility
4. Generate year-end tax report
5. Export for accountant
6. Retain digital copies for audit trail

## Security and Privacy

### Data Protection
- End-to-end encryption
- Secure cloud storage
- Regular backups
- Access controls

### Privacy Considerations
- Your data is yours
- No selling of information
- GDPR compliant
- Right to export or delete

### Retention Policies
- Keep receipts for recommended periods
- Tax receipts: 7 years (US) or local requirement
- Warranty receipts: Duration of warranty
- Business receipts: Per business policy
